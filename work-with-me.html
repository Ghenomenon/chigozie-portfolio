Questions

What most people ask.

Do I need to know anything technical?

No. I explain each chart in plain words and show your team how to use it.

What data do you need, and is it safe?

Usually an export from your spreadsheet, shop or accounting tool. My working terms above explain how I handle it.

How long does a project take?

A Starter takes about 1 week. A Standard takes about 2 weeks. I agree the timeline for a Full project after our call.

What if the first draft misses?

You see a draft in week 1. We review it together and I revise it. Changes beyond the agreed scope get a quote before I start.

Do I need Power BI licences?

I build in Power BI Desktop, which is free. Sharing reports online with your team may need Power BI licences. I tell you the cost before you commit.

Who owns the dashboard?

You do. You receive the report files at handover.

Which tools can you work with?

Excel and CSV files, SQL databases, and exports from your shop or accounting tool. If it can export a file, I can usually work with it.

Do you work outside Newcastle?

Yes. I work remotely across the UK by video call and email.
