How roles are scored

Fit runs from 0 to 5. It rewards roles that match your search, pay in your range, remote or local roles, and employers on the Home Office sponsor list. It cuts the score for pay below your floor.

Information only

Job Radar is not immigration advice. A licensed sponsor can still refuse a role. An employer missing from the register may still sponsor, because the register lists legal names. Salary thresholds depend on the job. Check gov.uk and the employer before you rely on anything here.
