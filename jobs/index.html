How roles are scored

Fit runs from 0 to 5. It rewards core analyst titles, pay in your range, remote or North East roles, and employers on the Home Office sponsor list. It cuts the score for pay below your floor. Roles that say no sponsorship never appear.

A role marked Not on register may still sponsor. The register lists legal names, so check the employer before you rule it out.
